# 🎮 Max Farm Smash — How to Play

You're in the browser and the game has loaded. Here's everything you need to know.

---

## The main menu

- **Play Game** — enter your name and start driving.
- **How to Play** — a quick in-game recap of the rules below.
- **Options** — truck color, round length, difficulty, enemy trucks, sound, minimap. See below.
- **Quit** — back out of the game.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `W` / `↑` | Drive forward |
| `S` / `↓` | Reverse |
| `A` / `←` | Turn left |
| `D` / `→` | Turn right |
| `Space` | Shoot |

## The goal

Survive until the timer runs out with the highest score possible. The round ends early if your health hits 0.

## What's on the farm

- **Animals** (cows, pigs, sheep, chickens) — smashing into these costs you **10 health** and gives no points. Avoid them, or use them to build toward Enemy MAX Mode (see below).
- **Trees** — crashing into one knocks you back and doesn't hurt you, but it's a solid obstacle, not a shortcut.
- **Vegetables** — drive over one to heal **25 health** (up to your max of 100).
- **Enemy trucks** — the real point-scorers. Shoot them or ram them.

## Fighting enemy trucks

- **Shooting** them is safer and does solid damage over a few hits.
- **Ramming** them does heavy damage to both sides — you'll hurt them a lot, but you'll take a big hit yourself, so use it when you're healthy.
- **Destroying one** earns **+200 points** and **+3 bonus seconds** on the clock.

## Health

You start with 100 health. Getting shot or rammed by an enemy truck costs health; running low is shown on the HUD in red as a warning. If your health hits 0, your truck is destroyed and the round ends immediately — start a new round to respawn.

## MAX Mode

Fighting, collecting vegetables, and smashing trees fills up your **MAX meter** (top-left HUD). Once it's full, you enter **MAX Mode** for 7 seconds:

- Your points are doubled.
- Your shots hit harder.
- Damage you take is reduced.

Bonus: crash into 3 animals in a row and every enemy truck enters **Enemy MAX Mode** for 3 seconds — they'll shoot faster and hit harder, so watch out.

## Playing with others

If other players are online at the same time, you'll see their trucks driving around the farm too — you can shoot or ram each other. Getting hit or rammed by another player costs health just like an enemy truck would. Scores from everyone playing feed into a shared leaderboard shown at the end of the round.

## The minimap

Bottom-left corner (can be turned off in Options):

- 🔵 Blue triangle — you
- ⚪ White dots — animals
- 🟢 Green dots — vegetables
- 🔴 Red dots — enemy trucks
- 🟡 Yellow dots — other players

## Options, explained

- **Truck color** — red, blue, green, orange, or purple.
- **Game time** — 20, 30, or 40 second rounds.
- **Difficulty** — easy, medium, hard, or expert (enemy trucks get more aggressive as this goes up, and as the round goes on).
- **Enemy trucks** — how many NPC trucks spawn, from 0 (just you and other players) up to 7.
- **Sound effects** / **Show minimap** — simple on/off toggles.

## Game over

When the round ends, you'll see your final score, your rank on the leaderboard, and how many trucks you destroyed. Hit **Play Again** to jump into a new round.
